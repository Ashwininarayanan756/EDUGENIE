| 4 | Social Impact / Customer Satisfaction | Improves financial awareness of students, reduces stress and helps them access affordable education |
| 5 | Business Model (Revenue Model) | Free basic plan; premium plan with advanced insights; partnerships with education platforms |
| 6 | Scalability of the Solution | Cloud deployment, multi-language support, mobile app and integration with UPI/bank data in future |
