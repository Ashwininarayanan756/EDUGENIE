| Stage | Customer Action | Touchpoint | Emotion | Pain Point | Opportunity (EduGenie.ai) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Hears about EduGenie.ai from friends or college | Social media, college notice | Curious | Unsure whether it is useful | Clear tagline and quick demo |
| Onboarding | Opens the app and enters profile, monthly income/allowance | Web app home page | Hopeful | Long forms are boring | Simple 3-field profile setup |
| Tracking | Adds daily expenses under categories | Expense entry page | Neutral | Forgetting to log expenses | Quick-add form and auto-categorisation |
| Planning | Sets monthly budget and savings goals | Budget planner | Motivated | Not knowing realistic limits | AI-suggested category limits |
| Getting Advice | Asks the AI assistant for savings tips, courses and scholarships | AI chat assistant | Excited | Generic advice | Personalised recommendations from own data |
| Review | Views charts and downloads report | Dashboard, report page | Satisfied | Hard to read data | Simple charts and summary insights |
| Retention | Returns every week to update and check goals | Alerts, dashboard | Confident | Losing interest | Overspending alerts and goal progress |
