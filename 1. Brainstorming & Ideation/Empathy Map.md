## Empathy Map – Target User: College Student

| Quadrant | Details |
|---|---|
| Says | "My money finishes too fast."; "I don't know where my allowance goes."; "I need cheap and good courses." |
| Thinks | "Am I spending too much?"; "Can I save for a laptop or course fee?"; "Is there any scholarship I can apply for?" |
| Does | Notes expenses in messages or notebooks; compares prices on many sites; asks friends for advice; skips tracking after a few days |
| Feels | Anxious at month-end, confused by too many options, motivated when able to save |
| Pains | No expense tracking habit, scattered information, unplanned spending, complex finance apps |
| Gains | Clear budget plan, savings goals, personalised recommendations, simple reports, less financial stress |
